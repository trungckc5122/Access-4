Step 2 – Writing Strategy
Blog Post Style Tips
  • In informal style (e.g. emails, blogs, postcards) you can omit sentence subjects I and we.
    Example: "We have finally arrived!" → "Finally arrived!"
  • Use present continuous for what's happening now: We're staying in…
  • Use past simple for what you've already done: We visited…
  • Add personal reactions: It's tiny!, Can't wait!

Step 4 – Useful Phrases & Sentence Structures
Blog Post Sentence Starters
1 Opening & greeting
  • Hi everyone! / Hello from [place]! / Just arrived in [place]!
2 Talking about your journey
  • We arrived [yesterday / this morning / last night] after a [long / short] journey.
  • The [flight / trip / journey] was [comfortable / tiring / bumpy] because…
3 Describing your accommodation
  • We're staying in [a small hotel / a guesthouse / a campsite] near [place].
  • It's [perfect / great] for [walking to the sights / relaxing / enjoying the view].
4 Describing the weather
  • The weather is [sunny / windy / cloudy / cool / warm] today.
  • It has been [hot / cold / rainy] since we arrived.
5 Talking about activities
  • This morning / Yesterday, we [visited / went to / explored]…
  • The view was [amazing / beautiful / disappointing].
  • I really enjoyed [the museum / the trip / the activity] because…
6 Future plans
  • Tomorrow we're going to [place / activity]. Can't wait!
  • Next, we're planning to… / We'll probably…
7 Closing
  • That's all for now. / I'll post more soon. / See you in the next post!
Step 5 – Writing Task
Your Blog Post
Write a blog post (100–120 words) including:
  • where you are and who with
  • the weather
  • activities you've done
  • plans for the next few days
Step 6 – Example Blog Post
Model Answer

Hi, everyone!

We arrived in Rome on Friday afternoon. The flight was smooth, and the weather here is warm and sunny. A bit hot during the day, but perfect in the evenings.

We've already visited the Colosseum. Took loads of photos and explored the old streets nearby. Also walked through Piazza Navona – such a beautiful square! Yesterday we went on a bus tour around the city and stopped at the Trevi Fountain. Threw a coin in for good luck!

Tomorrow we're going to visit the Vatican Museums and St. Peter's Basilica. Can't wait! On Monday, we're heading to the coast for a day at the beach. Will post again soon with more pictures!
