A Forum Post

I can write a forum post about a prank.

Task Description
You played a prank on a friend or family member. Write a forum post for an internet forum called 'Own Up!'
  • Give a short description of the prank.
  • Say how your friend or family member reacted.
  • Describe your feelings at the time.
  • Say whether you feel bad about it now, and why / why not.

Narrative Stage Grammar & Language Functions (A–D) Answer
Sentence Starters & Outline for the Forum Post
1 Short Description of the Prank (The Setup & Action)
  • It happened last week while my [friend/family member] was…
  • I decided to sneakily… / Before they arrived, I had already…
2 Reaction of the Target
  • At first, they looked completely confused, but then…
  • When they realized what happened, they turned cross / screamed out loud!
3 Your Feelings at the Time
  • I was absolutely delighted because it was so funny! / I grew anxious thinking I went too far.
4 Aftermath & Reflection (How you feel now)
  • Although it was hilarious at the time, I feel a bit bad now because…
  • Looking back, I wouldn't do it again because they are still suspicious of me!
Example Forum Post Model Answer

It was a warm Saturday afternoon, and Sam was sitting in the living room playing video games. He had left his glass of iced tea on the kitchen counter. I decided to sneakily replace the sugar in his glass with three big spoonfuls of salt. Before he walked back into the kitchen, I had already hidden behind the sofa to watch his reaction.

When he took a big gulp of the tea, his expression completely changed. At first, he looked totally confused, staring intensely at the glass. Then, he suddenly spit it out dramatically and turned cross, shouting my name across the house! My mom heard the noise and rushed in looking frightened, thinking something terrible had happened.

At the time, I was absolutely delighted and could not stop laughing. His shocked face made the whole plan worth it, and I felt weirdly proud of my clever setup. However, seeing how upset and disappointed he was afterward made me feel a bit bad about it later. Although we are laughing about it now, he checks all his drinks with suspicious eyes, so I definitely wouldn't do it again!
