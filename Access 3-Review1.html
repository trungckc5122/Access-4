Grammar Reference: Ôn tập
1. Relative Pronouns (Đại từ quan hệ) ▼
Đại từDùng choVí dụ
whongười (chủ ngữ)Shakespeare is the playwright who wrote Hamlet.
whichvật, đồ vậtA kilt is a skirt, which Scottish people wear.
wherenơi chốnParis is the city where the Eiffel Tower is.
whosesở hữu (của ai)Da Vinci, whose most famous painting is the Mona Lisa, was a painter.
Lưu ý: "who/which/where" thay thế cho danh từ đứng trước; "whose" thay thế cho tính từ sở hữu (his/her/its/their).

Down to Earth

The village of Matmata is on the slope of a hill in South Tunisia. Matmata is a very unusual place and a favorite tourist destination. Why? Because of its underground homes!

In Matmata, the Berber people build their homes under the ground. This is a one-thousand-year-old tradition. Today, there are more than 100 underground houses and there is even an underground hotel!

The climate in Tunisia is very hot, so living under the ground is a very good idea! The temperature is always the same. At night, when the desert is very cold, it is comfortably warm in a cave, and during the day when the sun shines down, the caves are cool. The houses are 16 to 32 feet deep and they have many small rooms used for sleeping, keeping food, and getting together. You can go from one room to another through small tunnels.

Life is simple here. The women stay at home and do household chores, while the men work on the land. A lot of people can show you around their houses. Do some of the Matmata houses seem familiar to you? Maybe they do because they appear in the movie Star Wars!
