Unusual Job: Pet Food Taster

A Not many people know that someone has to test the food before it is given to pets. Daniel Green (not his real name) works as a Pet Food Taster. He explains: "Most people think I just eat dog biscuits, but it's more complicated. My job is to check the quality, texture and sometimes the flavor of pet food before it reaches the shops."

B Daniel works for a large international company that produces food for cats and dogs. "Of course, the company has machines to test the nutrition, but they also need a human opinion. The reason is simple: animals can't tell us what the food tastes like. So, people like me are asked to describe the smell, consistency, and overall appeal."

C Although it may sound unpleasant, Daniel sometimes enjoys the job. "Some products taste better than people expect. A few are made from ingredients that humans also eat, like chicken or beef. I wouldn't choose it for dinner, but at least it isn't disgusting. Once I even said that a type of cat food tasted quite similar to pâté!"

D For every product he tries, Daniel has to complete a detailed report. "I give marks for appearance, smell, and flavor. I also write suggestions about how the food could be improved. After that, the company decides whether the recipe is ready to sell or not."

E Despite the unusual benefits, Daniel admits the job isn't easy. "Sometimes the food really tastes bad, and I have to force myself to try it. I'm also careful not to swallow much, because it isn't designed for people. And yes, my friends often laugh when I tell them what I do. But I don't mind—it's a unique career, and not many people can say they've tried the same things as I have!"
