Cookery schools for young people

A

Park Cookery School
Students of all ages are welcome at this school, and children can even do a course with their parents. Before starting each day, the group goes to the local market to buy fresh food to cook with, and at the end of the morning everyone has a meal together and shares the dishes they've made. Several times a year, the school asks a different professional chef to visit to give students ideas for new dishes. Book a course online at www.parkcookeryschool.co.uk.

B

Little Chefs Cookery School
The courses at this school show 10- to 16-year-olds how to make simple but delicious main courses and desserts. They prepare these meals using only fresh food bought from local farms. At the end of every day, students take photos of the dishes and post them on the school website. Then they carefully pack their dishes, so they can enjoy them later with their family that evening. For those busy students who cannot get there after school, there are also morning classes every Saturday.

C

Good Cook School
At this cooking school teenagers can find out how to cook meals which everyone in the family can enjoy. Students learn what they should and shouldn't eat and also how to cook with fruit and vegetables. The course includes online videos that give ideas for dishes students can practise at home at weekends. This school is very popular, so call them now to get your place!
