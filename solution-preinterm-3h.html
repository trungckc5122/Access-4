An Informal Letter

I can write an informal letter about a social activity.

1. Đề bài
You and a friend recently did a social activity together (e.g. going for a bike ride, playing basketball, meeting for a coffee, etc.). Write an informal letter to another friend. Follow the instructions below.
  • Say which activity you did and who with
  • Give your personal opinion about the activity
  • Mention what you did after the activity
  • Invite your friend to do a similar activity with you soon

6. Cách viết thư thân mật (Informal Letter)
  • Start with: Dear [Jake],
  • Begin the main part with a short introduction. You can use phrases like:
    How are you?    Sorry I didn't reply sooner.    Thanks for your letter.
  • Write in paragraphs.
  • Do not use very formal language in your letter.
  • Use contractions (it's, there's, etc.).
  • Finish the main part with a short paragraph. You can use phrases like:
    That's all for now.    I'd better go now.    Write again soon.
  • End the letter with Love, or Best wishes, and your first name.
7. Cấu trúc câu hữu ích
7.1. Kể hoạt động (What you did)
  • [Name] and I + V-ed + … → Kể đã làm gì cùng ai
    Tom and I went for a bike ride on Saturday.
  • We + V-ed + along / at / in + [place]
    We rode along the riverside trail.
7.2. Bày tỏ cảm nhận (Opinion)
  • I really enjoyed it – it was so + [adj]
    I really enjoyed the activity – it was so relaxing.
  • It was + [adj] + and a great way to + V
    It was exciting and a great way to get some exercise.
  • I felt + [adj] + but really + [adj]
    I felt tired but really happy afterwards.
7.3. Kể việc làm sau đó (After the activity)
  • After + [activity/V-ing], we + V-ed + …
    After the ride, we went to a small café and had some iced tea.
  • We talked for a while and just enjoyed the …
    We talked for a while and just enjoyed the afternoon.
7.4. Lời mời (Invitation)
  • Would you like to + V + …?
    Would you like to come next time?
  • I'd love for you to + V + …
    I'd love for you to join us!
  • We're planning + [activity] – why don't you join us?
    We're planning another ride this weekend – why don't you join us?
8. Sentence Starters
1 Opening
Dear [name],
How are you? / I wanted to tell you about something fun I did last weekend!
2 Kể hoạt động
[Name] and I went for a … on [day].
We … along / at / in [place], and it was amazing.
The weather was … and we saw / had …
3 Cảm nhận
I really enjoyed the activity – it was so … and a great way to …
I felt … but really … afterwards.
4 Sau hoạt động
After the …, we went to … and …
We talked for a while and just enjoyed the …
5 Lời mời
Would you like to come next time?
We're planning another … – I'd love for you to join us!
6 Closing
Write again soon! / That's all for now.
Best wishes, / Love, [Your Name]
9. Example Letter
Opening

Dear Jenny,

Introduction

How are you? I wanted to tell you about something fun I did last weekend!

Activity + Who with

Tom and I went for a bike ride on Saturday. We rode along the riverside trail, and it was amazing. The weather was sunny but not too hot, and we saw some beautiful views.

Opinion

I really enjoyed the activity – it was so relaxing and a great way to get some exercise.

After the activity

After the ride, we went to a small café and had some iced tea. We talked for a while and just enjoyed the afternoon.

Invitation

Would you like to come next time? We're planning another ride this weekend. I'd love for you to join us! Write again soon!

Closing

Best wishes,
Trung
