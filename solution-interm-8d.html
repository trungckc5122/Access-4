Grammar Reference: Câu Tường Thuật (Reported Speech)
I. Câu tường thuật dạng câu kể (Statements) ▼
Subject + say(s)/said (that) + Subject + Verb
Subject + tell(s)/told + Object (that) + Subject + Verb
Bước 1: Chọn từ tường thuật

Ta bắt buộc phải dùng "told" khi trong câu trực tiếp xuất hiện người nghe, hoặc có thể thay thế "told" bằng "said to". Nếu trong câu không có đối tượng nghe thì ta sử dụng "said".

"You are late for school." Tom said to Lan.
→ Tom told/said to Lan that she was late for school.
Bước 2: "Lùi thì" động từ trong câu trực tiếp
Direct speech Reported speech Example
present simple past simple 'I need a credit card,' said Tim. → Tim said he needed a credit card.
present continuous past continuous 'I'm taking Lizzie shopping,' said Tim. → Tim said he was taking Lizzie shopping.
present perfect simple past perfect simple 'I've bought Tom a present,' said Tim. → Tim said he'd bought Tom a present.
present perfect continuous past perfect continuous 'I've been thinking about buying a car,' said Tim. → Tim said he'd been thinking about buying a car.
past simple past perfect simple 'I spent six euros,' said Tim. → Tim said he'd spent six euros.
past continuous past perfect continuous 'I was hoping to find a new top,' said Tim. → Tim said he'd been hoping to find a new top.
past perfect simple past perfect simple (no tense change) 'I'd looked everywhere for my credit card before I found it,' said Tim. → Tim said he'd looked everywhere for his credit card before he found it.
past perfect continuous past perfect continuous (no tense change) 'I'd been looking for that book for weeks before I found it,' said Tim. → Tim said he'd been looking for that book for weeks before he found it.
am/is/are going to was/were going to 'I'm going to go shopping,' said Tim. → Tim said he was going to go shopping.
will would 'I'll need a credit card,' said Tim. → Tim said he would need a credit card.
can could 'I can take Lizzie shopping,' said Tim. → Tim said he could take Lizzie shopping.
must/have to had to 'I must go to the supermarket,' said Tim. → Tim said he had to go to the supermarket.
may might 'I may go shopping later,' said Tim. → Tim said he might go shopping later.

(*) Một số trường hợp không cần lùi thì trong câu tường thuật:

  • Động từ tường thuật trong câu được dùng ở thì hiện tại: không cần lùi thì trong những câu sử dụng động từ tường thuật ở thì hiện tại hoặc câu gốc đang nói về một sự thật ở hiện tại.
    Vd: He says he has missed the train, but he'll catch the next one.
  • Câu nói trực tiếp thể hiện một chân lý, một sự thật hiển nhiên.
    Vd: Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.
  • Câu trực tiếp ở dạng câu điều kiện loại 2 hoặc câu điều kiện loại 3.
    Vd: She said if she won the lottery, she would travel around Europe.
  • Không lùi thì khi mệnh đề nằm sau "wish" diễn tả một mong muốn có thể thực hiện được ở hiện tại.
    Vd: She wished she could go to the beach the next weekend.

a. Đại từ nhân xưng b. Đại từ sở hữu c. Tính từ sở hữu
I → he, she
we → they
you → I, we
me → him, her
us → them
you → me, us		 mine → his, hers
ours → theirs
yours → mine, ours		 my → his, her
our → their

★ Lưu ý: Khi tường thuật lại lời nói của chính mình, đại từ và tính từ sở hữu không đổi.
Ex: I said, 'I like my new house.' → I said (that) I liked my new house. (Tôi nói rằng tôi thích ngôi nhà mới của mình.)

Bước 4: Biến đổi trạng ngữ chỉ thời gian, nơi chốn
Direct speech Reported speech Example
here there 'I usually shop here,' said Tim. → Tim said he usually shopped there.
now / at the moment then / at that moment 'I'm shopping at the moment,' said Tim. → Tim said he was shopping then/at that moment.
tomorrow the next/following day 'I'm going shopping tomorrow,' said Tim. → Tim said he was going shopping the next/following day.
tonight that night 'I'm going shopping tonight,' said Tim. → Tim said he was going shopping that night.
next week/month/year the following week/month/year 'I'm going shopping next week,' said Tim. → Tim said he was going shopping the following week.
yesterday the day before / the previous day 'I went shopping yesterday,' said Tim. → Tim said he'd been shopping the day before/the previous day.
last week/month/year the week/month/year before / the previous week/month/year 'I went shopping last week,' said Tim. → Tim said he'd been shopping the week before/the previous week.
ago before / previously 'I went shopping two days ago,' said Tim. → Tim said he'd been shopping two days before/previously.
- He said to me: "I didn't finish my homework yesterday." → He told me that he hadn't finished his homework the day before.
- "I will see this movie with my girlfriend tomorrow," Nam said. → Nam said that he would see that movie with his girlfriend the following day.
- Mary said: "This is the first time I've broken my arm." → Mary said that that was the first time she had broken her arm.
II. Câu tường thuật dạng câu hỏi (Questions) ▼
1. Yes/No Question

Câu hỏi Yes/No question là dạng câu hỏi đơn giản trong tiếng Anh, thường bắt đầu bằng động từ "be" hoặc trợ động từ.

S + asked (+O) / wanted to know / wondered + if/whether + S + V
"Are you angry?" he asked. → He asked if/whether I was angry.

* Khi tường thuật câu hỏi Có hay Không (Yes-No questions), ta phải chuyển câu hỏi trực tiếp sang dạng khẳng định, rồi thực hiện thay đổi thì, trạng từ chỉ thời gian, trạng từ chỉ nơi chốn, đại từ chỉ định, và chủ ngữ, tân ngữ, đại từ sở hữu cho phù hợp.

* Động từ tường thuật:
says/say to + O → asks / ask + O
said to + O → asked + O

He said to me: "Have you been to America?" → He asked me if/whether I had been to America.
2. Wh-Question

Cách làm vẫn tương tự như 4 bước chuyển sang câu tường thuật trong tiếng Anh, tuy nhiên cần lưu ý những điều sau:

  • Lặp lại từ để hỏi sau động từ giới thiệu
  • Đổi trật tự chủ ngữ - động từ sau từ để hỏi thành câu tường thuật
S + asked (+O) / wanted to know / wondered + Wh-word + S + V
My mother said, 'What time do you go to bed?' → My mother wanted to know/asked me what time I went to bed.
III. Câu tường thuật dạng câu mệnh lệnh (Commands/Orders/Requests) ▼
Subject + asked/told + Object (+ not) + to-infinitive
"Close the door," he said. (Anh ấy nói: "Đóng cửa lại.") → He told me to close the door. (Anh ấy bảo tôi đóng cửa lại.)
"Please close the door," she said to me. (Cô ấy nói với tôi: "Làm ơn đóng cửa lại.") → She asked me to close the door. (Cô ấy yêu cầu tôi đóng cửa lại.)
