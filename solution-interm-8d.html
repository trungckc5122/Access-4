I can report questions correctly.

Ngữ pháp: Câu hỏi tường thuật (Reported questions)
1. Quy tắc chuyển câu hỏi tường thuật ▼
Cấu trúc chung:
  • asked + object + (if / whether) + clause (cho câu hỏi Yes/No)
  • asked + object + wh-word + clause (cho câu hỏi Wh-)
Quy tắc lùi thì (Tense changes):
  • present simple → past simple ("Are you tired?" → He asked if I was tired.)
  • present continuous → past continuous ("What are you doing?" → She asked what I was doing.)
  • past simple → past perfect ("Did you go?" → He asked if I had gone.)
  • present perfect → past perfect ("Have you seen it?" → She asked if I had seen it.)
  • will → would ("Will you come?" → He asked if I would come.)
  • can → could ("Can you help?" → She asked if I could help.)
Thay đổi từ chỉ thời gian và nơi chốn:
  • today → that day
  • yesterday → the day before
  • tomorrow → the next day
  • here → there
  • now → then
"Where do you live?" → He asked me where I lived.
"Have you finished?" → She asked if I had finished.
"Will you be there?" → They asked if I would be there.
2. So sánh said vs asked ▼
  • said + (that) + mệnh đề (dùng cho câu trần thuật)
  • asked + object + if / wh-word + mệnh đề (dùng cho câu hỏi)
  • Không dùng said cho câu hỏi tường thuật.
"I'm tired." → He said he was tired.
"Are you tired?" → He asked if I was tired.
