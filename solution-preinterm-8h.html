Writing an Email

I can write an email about a holiday in London.

Task
Imagine you have just returned home from a holiday in London with your family. Write an email to your English friend Sarah in which you:
  • describe the journey
  • describe a tourist attraction you visited
  • describe a crime you saw taking place
  • ask for some information
Paragraph Plan
Paragraph Content Notes
1 The journey Transport, weather, time, feelings
2 Tourist attraction What, where, description, adjectives
3 Crime What happened, where, what happened next
4 Request for information What you want to know, reason

Guiding Questions Sample Ideas How to Build the Story (Cách phát triển câu chuyện)
Where did you go? What did you see? How did you feel? The British Museum, Ancient Egypt section, amazed
Introduction (Giới thiệu địa điểm và ấn tượng chung): One of the most interesting places we visited was the British Museum.
Body (Mô tả chi tiết và cảm xúc): It was huge and full of amazing exhibits. I especially loved the Ancient Egypt section with the mummies and old statues.
Ending (Kết thúc bằng cảm nghĩ hoặc điều nhớ nhất): It was amazing!
What happened? How did you react? What was the result? Shopping near Oxford Street, thief, handbag, police caught him, people relieved
Introduction (Giới thiệu tình huống bất ngờ xảy ra): Something unexpected happened, though.
Body (Kể lại diễn biến sự việc): While we were shopping near Oxford Street, I saw a man grab a woman's handbag and run away.
Ending (Nêu kết quả và cảm xúc của mọi người): Fortunately, a police officer caught him quickly, and everyone around was relieved.
Sentence Structures
Purpose Structure Example
Describing a journey We travelled by … → The weather was … → It took … hours. We travelled by plane and the flight took about 13 hours.
Describing a place We visited … → It was (adj) … / I was amazed by … We visited the British Museum. It was enormous and fascinating.
Giving opinion or feeling I really enjoyed … / It was exciting to … / I felt … I felt relieved after the police officer caught him.
Describing an event I saw … → Suddenly … → Luckily, … / Unfortunately, … I saw a man stealing a handbag. Luckily, the police caught him quickly / Unfortunately, he was able to escape.
Asking for information By the way, do you know …? / Can you tell me …? / I'd like to know … By the way, can you tell me any good places to stay in London?
Model Email

Hi Sarah,

How are you? I've just returned from my holiday in London with my family and wanted to tell you all about it.

We travelled by plane from Ho Chi Minh City, and the journey took around thirteen hours. The flight was smooth, and the weather was lovely when we arrived. I felt a bit tired but really excited to explore the city.

One of the most interesting places we visited was the British Museum. It was huge and full of amazing exhibits. I especially loved the Ancient Egypt section with the mummies and old statues. It was amazing!

Something unexpected happened, though. While we were shopping near Oxford Street, I saw a man grab a woman's handbag and run away. Fortunately, a police officer caught him quickly, and everyone around was relieved.

By the way, I'm planning to visit London again next summer. Do you know any nice hotels near the city centre?

Take care and write back soon,
Trung
