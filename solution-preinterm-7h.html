Writing

An Opinion Essay

I can write an essay suggesting how to spend €1 million.

1 – Writing Strategy / Kỹ thuật viết
Key Writing Techniques
Avoid starting too many sentences the same way.
Use a variety of expressions:
  • Giving opinions: It seems to me … / From my point of view … / I believe …
  • Adding ideas: Moreover … / Furthermore … / In addition …
Use conditional sentences in different orders:
  • If I had €1 million, I would …
  • I would … if I had €1 million.
Use concession clauses:
  • Although it might not solve every problem, …
  • Even though this would be expensive, …
Task: Imagine you had €1 million to spend, but could not spend any of it on yourself. Think of three ways to spend it. Answer the questions below to help you develop your ideas.
  • Include an introduction and a conclusion like the model in exercise 2.
  • Say what you would spend half the money on.
  • Choose two more ways to spend the rest.
  • Explain what effect the money would have.
  • Give reasons for your choices.
2 – Model Essay / Bài mẫu
Sample Answer

If I had €1 million, I would try to spend it in ways that could help as many people as possible. In my opinion, this would be a meaningful opportunity to give something back to society.

To begin with, I would spend half of the money on building new classrooms in poor rural areas. Many children still study in overcrowded rooms, and better facilities would give them a real chance to learn effectively. If they had more space and better conditions, they would find it easier to succeed in their education.

Next, I would donate a quarter of the money to an international charity that provides clean drinking water. This is important because without safe water, people face serious health risks. Although this donation might not solve every problem, it could save lives and improve health in many communities. For instance, charities such as WaterAid have already helped thousands of families in Africa by building wells.

Finally, I would use the last quarter to support a local sports club. This would give young people a safe place to play and stay away from trouble. If they had more chances to do sports, they would develop healthier lifestyles. For example, teenagers who join football or volleyball clubs often show more confidence and discipline.

In conclusion, I would use the money to improve education, provide clean water, and support healthy lifestyles. These three choices would not only change individual lives but also make society stronger and more hopeful.

    5 – PEE Practice / Building Ideas (Outline) / Dàn ý
    PEE Outline Table

    Complete the PEE table for your essay. Press 👁️ to see a hint for each cell.

    Point (What?) Explain (Why?) Example (How?)
    Guiding Questions (PEE):
    • Point (What?)
      • What is your idea to spend the money on?
      • Who would receive the money (e.g. school, hospital, charity, sports club)?
    • Explain (Why?)
      • Why is this important?
      • What problem does this solve?
      • How would people's lives improve?
    • Example (How?)
      • Can you think of a real example of this situation?
      • If not, can you imagine what could happen if this money was given?
      • What results or changes would you expect?
    Useful Phrases for the Essay
    Introduction
    • If I had €1 million, I would try to spend it in ways that …
    • In my opinion, this would be a meaningful opportunity to …
    • From my point of view, the best way to use this money would be …
    Body – Adding points
    • To begin with, I would spend … on …
    • Next / Furthermore / Moreover, I would …
    • Finally, I would use the last … to …
    Body – Explaining & giving examples
    • This is important because …
    • If they had …, they would …
    • Although this might not solve every problem, it could …
    • For instance / For example, …
    Conclusion
    • In conclusion, I would use the money to …
    • These choices would not only … but also …
    • It seems to me that … is the most important because …
