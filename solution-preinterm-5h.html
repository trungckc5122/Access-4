    Structure of the Application Letter
    PhầnNội dung & cụm từ mẫu
    Greeting Dear Sir/Madam,
    Dear Mr./Ms. [Last name], (if you know it)
    Paragraph 1 – Purpose I am writing to apply for the position of …, as advertised in/on …
    Paragraph 2 – Personal info & experience I am … years old and I am studying at …
    I have experience in …
    I am … (positive adjectives) and have … (skills/qualities)
    I am a responsible / friendly / organized / creative person.
    I am available on weekends / after school / in the evenings.
    Paragraph 3 – Availability & closing I have enclosed my CV for your consideration.
    I am available for an interview …
    Thank you for your time and consideration.
    I look forward to hearing from you.
    Closing Yours sincerely,
    [Your name]
    Cấu trúc câu hữu ích
    Paragraph 1 – Nêu mục đích
    • I am writing to apply for the position of [job], as advertised [where].
      I am writing to apply for the position of Music Club Assistant at J97 Entertainment, as advertised on the club's noticeboard.
    Paragraph 2 – Giới thiệu bản thân
    • I am [age] years old and currently a student at [school].
    • I have a strong interest in … and have been [V-ing] for …
      I have a strong interest in music and have been learning to play the guitar for two years.
    • I consider myself [adj] and [adj].
      I consider myself an organized and responsible person.
    • I am available on [days] / after school / in the evenings.
    Paragraph 3 – Kết thúc
    • I have enclosed my CV with further details.
    • I would be happy to attend an interview if required.
    • Thank you for considering my application.
    • I look forward to hearing from you.
    Sentence Starters
    1 Greeting
    Dear Mr./Ms. [Last name], / Dear Sir/Madam,
    2 Purpose
    I am writing to apply for the position of …, as advertised on/in …
    3 Personal Info
    I am … years old and currently a student at … School.
    I have experience in … / I have been [V-ing] for …
    I consider myself … and … / I am a … person.
    4 Availability
    I am available on [days] / after school / in the evenings.
    5 Closing
    I have enclosed my CV with further details and would be happy to attend an interview if required.
    Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to hearing from you.
    Yours sincerely, / [Your name]
    Sample Answer

    Dear Mr. Trinh,

    I am writing to apply for the position of Music Club Assistant at J97 Entertainment, as advertised on the club's noticeboard.

    I am 14 years old and currently a student at Hiep Binh Secondary School. I have a strong interest in music and have been learning to play the guitar for two years. I often assist my classmates during music lessons, and I enjoy working in groups. I consider myself an organized and responsible person, and I would be happy to help set up instruments and prepare materials for rehearsals.

    I am available on Tuesdays and Thursdays after school. I have enclosed my CV with further details and would be happy to attend an interview if required. Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to hearing from you.

    Yours sincerely,
    Trung
