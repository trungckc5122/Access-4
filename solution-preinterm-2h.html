Email Writing Task – Invitation to Extreme Competitions

You saw an advertisement. Write an invitation email to your friend Sam. Include:

  • What the event is
  • The date, the time, and the place
  • Any instructions (what Sam should bring, wear, etc.)

Write an informal letter

Extreme Competitions!

Do you like kayaking, paintballing, orienteering or quad biking?

Come to Denby Park Estate on 2 May.

We are holding competitions in each of these extreme sports.
Be prepared for a day full of adventure.
Arrive at 9 a.m. Stay till late!

Great prizes for the winners of each competition!

Useful English structures for describing "bring or carry something with you"
  • You need a / an + [item] to …V0…… → bạn cần mang theo…để………
  • Don't forget your + [item] because …S+V+O….. → đừng quên mang theo…bởi vì
  • We will need + [item] to …..V0…… → chúng ta sẽ cần mang theo…để….
  • We should wear a + [item] to ….V0… → chúng ta nên mang (mặc, đội)…để….
  • Wearing a + [item] is important because …S+V+O….. → mặc / đội món này là quan trọng…bởi vì…..
Making suggestions
  • Shall we (do something)?
  • Let's (do something).
  • Do you fancy (doing something)?
  • How / What about (doing something)?
  • We could always (do something).

Example Email

Hi Sam,

Guess what? I saw an ad for some amazing extreme sports competitions at Denby Park Estate on 2 May! They'll have kayaking, paintballing, orienteering, and quad biking. It all starts at 9 a.m., and we can stay until late!

Let's go kayaking because it is great to enjoy the water and try a new extreme sport like this. You should bring sporty clothes, comfy shoes, and maybe an extra set of clothes in case we get wet or muddy.

It's going to be an awesome day full of fun and adventure! I think you'll love it.

Let me know if you want to join!

Cheers,
[Your Name]
