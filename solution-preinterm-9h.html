Writing a Letter of Complaint

I can write a letter of complaint about a council decision.

Task
Your local council has decided to stop giving under-18s cheap travel on buses. Write a letter of complaint about this decision, explaining why it is bad for teenagers and for the environment.
Paragraph Plan
Paragraph Content Notes / Guiding ideas
1 Reason for writing Express disappointment and introduce the issue
2 Negative effects on teenagers Explain how the decision affects students' daily life
3 Negative effects on the environment Explain how the decision harms the environment
4 Conclusion Summarize your opinion and suggest reconsideration
Cách xây dựng ý tưởng thành đoạn văn (P–E–E: Point – Explain – Expand/Example)

Sentence Structures
Purpose Structure Example
1. Expressing disappointment I am writing to express my concern about... / I was disappointed to hear that... I am writing to express my concern about the council's decision to stop cheap bus travel for under-18s.
2. Giving the main reason The main reason I object to this decision is that... The main reason I object to this decision is that it affects students' ability to travel to school.
3. Giving explanation This is because... / The reason is that... This is because many students depend on cheap bus fares to get to school every day. The reason is that higher bus prices will make travel too expensive for families.
4. Giving consequences This will lead to... / As a result,... As a result, many young people will find it harder to join school activities.
5. Adding another main idea Additionally, ... / In addition, ... Additionally, this decision will also have negative effects on the environment.
6. Summing up For these reasons, I strongly believe that... For these reasons, I strongly believe that the council should reconsider its decision.
Model Letter

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my concern about the council's recent decision to stop offering cheap bus travel for under-18s. This change will seriously affect many young people in our community.

Firstly, this decision is unfair to young people. This is because many students rely on cheap bus fares to get to school every day, and without this support, travelling will become too expensive for many families. As a result, some students might have to walk long distances or even miss after-school activities, which limits their opportunities.

Additionally, this rule will harm the environment. The reason is that if fewer teenagers use buses, more families will drive their children to school, which means heavier traffic and higher fuel use. As a result, there will be more air pollution, and our town will become less eco-friendly.

For these reasons, I strongly believe that the council should reconsider its decision and continue providing cheap bus travel for under-18s.

Yours faithfully,
Trung

Idea Brainstorming
Negative Aspect Guiding questions theo cấu trúc P–E–E Your ideas
1. Teenagers' daily life Point: What problems will students face when bus fares increase?
Explain: Why do teenagers rely on cheap bus travel every day? (e.g. no other cheap transport support, cost a lot without support)
Expand/Example: What might happen if they can't afford it (e.g. arrive late, skip clubs, walk long distances)?
Gợi ý P–E–E:
Point: Teenagers will find it difficult to travel to school and activities daily.
Explain: This is because they do not have their own income and there are no other cheap alternatives for public transport.
Expand/Example: For instance, many students will arrive late to school or have to cancel their after-school sports clubs.
2. Financial difficulty for families Point: How will this decision affect families financially? (e.g. pay more for daily travel)
Explain: Why is paying full fare a burden for low-income families? (e.g. families with many children)
Expand/Example: What could families be forced to give up to cover transport costs? (e.g. food, clothes, school supplies)
Gợi ý P–E–E:
Point: This change will put a heavy financial burden on many families.
Explain: Parents with multiple children will have to spend a significant portion of their income on basic daily travel.
Expand/Example: Consequently, low-income families might have to cut down on food, clothes, or essential school supplies to cover transport costs.
3. Equality and fairness Point: Who does this decision affect most? (e.g. poorer families, not rich ones)
Explain: Why are poorer people more affected? (e.g. buses are their main transport)
Expand/Example: Why is this unfair? (e.g. rich people drive cars, poor people lose benefits)
Gợi ý P–E–E:
Point: This decision is unfair and increases inequality in the community.
Explain: Wealthier families can easily drive their kids to school, whereas poorer families rely completely on public buses.
Expand/Example: This is highly unfair because it takes away travel opportunities specifically from disadvantaged children.
4. Environmental pollution Point: How will this decision affect the environment? (e.g. more cars on the road)
Explain: What happens if fewer teenagers use buses?
Expand/Example: What problems might this cause?
Gợi ý P–E–E:
Point: Stopping cheap bus travel will harm the environment.
Explain: Without cheap buses, more parents will drive their children to school individually instead of letting them ride the bus.
Expand/Example: This will lead to more private cars on the road, causing worse traffic congestion and higher air pollution in the town.
💡 Lưu ý khi lập dàn ý (Idea Brainstorming):
  • Chọn 1 trong các ý 1–3 để trả lời các câu hỏi nhằm xây dựng nội dung thể hiện tác động tiêu cực lên giới trẻ (Paragraph 2).
  • Trả lời các câu hỏi ở ý số 4 để xây dựng nội dung thể hiện tác động tiêu cực lên môi trường (Paragraph 3).
