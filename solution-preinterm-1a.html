Vocabulary Reference
List of Adjectives (Feelings) ▼
  • Anxious /’æŋkʃəs/: lo lắng, bồn chồn (về điều gì đó sắp tới).
  • Delighted /dɪˈlaɪtɪd/: cực kỳ vui mừng, hạnh phúc.
  • Embarrassed /ɪmˈbærəst/: xấu hổ, ngượng ngùng (do làm sai hoặc bị chú ý).
  • Frightened /ˈfraɪtənd/: sợ hãi.
  • Proud /praʊd/: tự hào.
  • Relieved /rɪˈliːvd/: nhẹ nhõm (khi một điều tệ hại đã qua hoặc không xảy ra).
  • Shocked /ʃɒkt/: sửng sốt, cú sốc.
  • Upset /ʌpˈsɛt/: buồn bã, thất vọng hoặc bực bội.
  • Envious /ˈɛnviəs/: ghen tị.
  • Suspicious /səˈspɪʃəs/: nghi ngờ.
  • Ashamed /əˈʃeɪmd/: hổ thẹn (do cảm thấy mình làm điều sai trái).
  • Cross /krɒs/: bực mình, tức giận.

4. 🎧 1.02 Listen. How are the people feeling? Choose from the adjectives below.
Audio 1.02

Audio Transcript

Speaker 1: The other team weren't very good and we quickly scored three goals. But then we relaxed, I think. We thought we were sure to win. And they scored three goals too, so it was 3–3. But luckily we managed to score in the last minute and win the match. I was so relieved!

Speaker 2: Mark was invited to a party at the weekend, but he told me he was too tired to go. But then Sally saw him in town with Lizzie. That's what she said, anyway. So, did he stay in? I don't know. It made me feel very suspicious.

Speaker 3: There's a boy in my class who's good at everything: sports, music, languages... He's also captain of the football team and really popular. I can't help feeling envious sometimes. How does he do it?!

Speaker 4: I had a really bad argument with my sister last night. I said some really horrible things to her. Now I feel so ashamed about it. I'm going to apologise straight away when I get home.

Answers

Speaker 1:
Đáp án: relieved
Bằng chứng: "But luckily we managed to score in the last minute and win the match."
Giải thích: Họ suýt thua (3-3) nhưng thắng vào phút cuối nên cảm thấy relieved (nhẹ nhõm).
Speaker 2:
Đáp án: suspicious
Bằng chứng: "But then Sally saw him in town with Lizzie. That's what she said, anyway. So, did he stay in? I don't know."
Giải thích: Bạn trai bảo ở nhà nhưng lại bị thấy đi chơi với người khác, cô gái cảm thấy suspicious (nghi ngờ).
Speaker 3:
Đáp án: envious
Bằng chứng: "There's a boy in my class who's good at everything... captain of the football team... good-looking and popular... How does he do it?!"
Giải thích: Ghen tị với cậu bé giỏi mọi thứ mà không cần cố gắng: envious.
Speaker 4:
Đáp án: ashamed
Bằng chứng: "I had a really bad argument with my sister... I feel so bad about it. I'm going to apologise straight away..."
Giải thích: Cảm thấy tồi tệ và tội lỗi vì đã cãi nhau vớ vẩn: ashamed (hổ thẹn).
