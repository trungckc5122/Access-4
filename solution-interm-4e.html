Vocabulary Reference & Vocab Boost!
1. Using do, make and take ▼

do, make and take are very common verbs that appear in many fixed collocations.

  • DO → work, activities: do homework, do yoga, do your best
  • MAKE → create, produce: make bread, make an appointment, make sense
  • TAKE → receive, hold, use: take a photo, take your time, take painkillers

💡 TIP:

  • DO → action
  • MAKE → create
  • TAKE → receive
