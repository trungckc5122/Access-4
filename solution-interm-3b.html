Comparison

I can make comparisons using a variety of structures.

Ngữ pháp: Các cấu trúc so sánh (Comparison)
1. So sánh hơn & So sánh nhất (Comparative & Superlative) ▼
Cấu trúc so sánh hơn (Comparative):
  • Tính từ ngắn: adjective + -er + than
    John is taller than Paul.
  • Tính từ dài: more + adjective + than
    This book is more interesting than that one.
  • So sánh hơn với trạng từ: more + adverb + than
    She speaks more fluently than her brother.
Cấu trúc so sánh nhất (Superlative):
  • Tính từ ngắn: the + adjective + -est
    Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world.
  • Tính từ dài: the most + adjective
    This is the most expensive hotel in the city.
  • So sánh nhất với trạng từ: the most + adverb
    Of all the students, she speaks English the most fluently.
This is the best book I've ever read. (bất quy tắc)
She runs faster than me.
He's the most talented musician in the band.
2. So sánh kép (Double comparative) ▼
Cấu trúc 1: more and more / -er and -er (càng ngày càng...)
  • Diễn tả sự thay đổi liên tục.
    It's getting colder and colder. (Trời đang ngày càng lạnh hơn.)
  • She is becoming more and more confident. (Cô ấy càng ngày càng tự tin.)
Cấu trúc 2: The + comparative, the + comparative (càng...càng...)
  • Diễn tả mối quan hệ nhân quả giữa hai hành động.
    The harder you study, the better you will do.
  • The more you practise, the more confident you become.
The weather is getting worse and worse.
The faster you drive, the more likely you are to have an accident.
3. Các cấu trúc so sánh khác ▼
as...as (bằng nhau):
  • She is as tall as her sister.
  • This area isn't as popular as it used to be.
less / the least (so sánh về mức độ):
  • less + adjective + than (ít hơn)
    My flat is less spacious than yours.
  • the least + adjective (ít nhất)
    The kitchen is the least spacious room in the house.
fewer / the fewest (so sánh về số lượng đếm được):
  • fewer + danh từ đếm được số nhiều + than
    There are fewer flowers in the flower bed this year than last.
  • the fewest + danh từ đếm được số nhiều
    This house has got the fewest rooms of the three.
This area isn't as popular as it was five years ago.
Wales is less remote than the north of Scotland.
Which room has got the fewest pieces of furniture?
4. Lỗi thường gặp (Common Mistakes) ▼
  • ❌ This is the most best film. → ✅ This is the best film.
    Không dùng most với tính từ bất quy tắc (best).
  • ❌ He is more taller than me. → ✅ He is taller than me.
    Không dùng more với tính từ ngắn.
  • ❌ She sings beautifuler than him. → ✅ She sings more beautifully than him.
    Với trạng từ dài, dùng more + adverb.
  • ❌ The more you eat, the more you fat. → ✅ The more you eat, the fatter you get.
    Sử dụng cấu trúc the + comparative, the + comparative đúng.
